📘 Engineering Governing Equations

• Reynolds Number: Re = (ρ · v · D) / μ (Laminar if Re < 2300, Turbulent if Re ≥ 4000)

• Darcy Friction Factor: Laminar: f = 64 / Re | Turbulent: Swamee-Jain approximation of Colebrook-White equation

• Darcy-Weisbach Major Loss: h_f = f · (L / D) · (v² / 2g)

• Minor Fitting Loss: h_m = ΣK · (v² / 2g)

• Hydraulic Power: P_hyd = Q · ΔP = Q · ρ · g · h_total
